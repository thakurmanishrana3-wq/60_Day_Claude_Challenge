Prior Authorization is one of the most frustrating parts of US healthcare — and most people have no idea how it works.

So I built an interactive, gamified simulator that walks you through the ENTIRE PA process — drag-and-drop style.

**What's inside:**
🧑‍⚕️ 3 workflow lanes — Patient · Provider · Payer
📋 4 real patient scenarios (knee surgery, brain MRI, Humira, CHF admission)
📄 Document collection, medical necessity evaluation, PA submission
⚖️ Payer outcomes — Approve · Deny · Pend · Appeal · Peer-to-Peer Review
🎯 Efficiency score + simulated days elapsed
🎉 Celebration animation on approval
📚 Educational explanations after every single step

Every action teaches you something real — ICD-10 codes, step therapy, InterQual criteria, the two-midnight rule.

**The wildest facts I learned building this:**
→ 35 million PAs are submitted every year in the US
→ 90% of appealed denials eventually get overturned
→ Peer-to-Peer reviews flip decisions 50–70% of the time
→ Electronic PA cuts submission time from 90 min → 10 min

Built with a single HTML file. No frameworks, no CDN, no backend.

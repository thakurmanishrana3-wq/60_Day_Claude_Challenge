## 🏭 AI Supply Chain Control Tower — Day 31

### What's built

A fully offline, single-file HTML game where you play the **Head of Operations** at a global supply chain company. Every second counts, every alert is real, and every wrong decision tanks your KPIs.

---

### Game features

**7 Live KPIs** — Service Level, Customer Satisfaction, Inventory Health, Transport Efficiency, Operating Cost, Revenue Protected, and Score — all updating in real time from your decisions.

**10 Alert Types** — Port Congestion, Supplier Delay, Truck Breakdown, Warehouse Stockout, Customs Inspection, Demand Spike, Machine Failure, Weather Disruption, Inventory Discrepancy, Damaged Shipment. Each with realistic descriptions and business impact.

**4 Action Qualities per Alert** — Best / OK / Risky / Bad — color coded green/blue/orange/red, with different KPI consequences and score multipliers.

**Escalating difficulty** — Alert spawn interval drops from 12s → 4s over 3 minutes. Unresolved alerts expire and penalize KPIs. Up to 6 simultaneous active alerts.

**Live Risk Gauge** — SVG semicircle gauge with animated needle tracking operational pressure.

**Event Log** — Timestamped scrolling log of every decision and expiry.

**End Screen** — Grade A+ through D, final KPI report, decision accuracy breakdown, and a personalized operational summary.

**Extra:** Pause (Space bar too), Help modal, Sound toggle, responsive layout.
